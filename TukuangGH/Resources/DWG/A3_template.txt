# Sample A3 Frame Template

This file represents a template for A3 paper frame (297 × 420 mm).

## Frame Components

### Outer Border
- Rectangle from (0,0) to (297,420)

### Inner Margin  
- Rectangle from (10,10) to (287,410)
- 10mm margin from all edges

### Title Block
- Rectangle from (222,0) to (297,63)
- Size: 75mm × 63mm (approximately 25% × 15% of page)

### Title Block Subdivisions
- Horizontal lines at y=15.75, 31.5, 47.25
- Vertical line at x=259.5 (middle of title block)

### Revision Triangle
- Triangle at top-right corner
- Points: (277,420), (297,420), (297,400)

### Measurement Marks
- Marks every 100mm along edges
- Bottom edge: marks at x=100, 200
- Top edge: marks at x=100, 200  
- Left edge: marks at y=100, 200, 300, 400
- Right edge: marks at y=100, 200, 300, 400
- Mark size: 5mm

## Usage
Place this template as "A3.dwg" or "A3.3dm" in the Resources/DWG folder.
The component will automatically load and use this template for A3 frames.
