# Sample A4 Frame Template

This file represents a template for A4 paper frame (210 × 297 mm).

## Frame Components

### Outer Border
- Rectangle from (0,0) to (210,297)

### Inner Margin  
- Rectangle from (10,10) to (200,287)
- 10mm margin from all edges

### Title Block
- Rectangle from (157,0) to (210,44)
- Size: 53mm × 44mm (approximately 25% × 15% of page)

### Title Block Subdivisions
- Horizontal lines at y=11, 22, 33
- Vertical line at x=183.5 (middle of title block)

### Revision Triangle
- Triangle at top-right corner
- Points: (190,297), (210,297), (210,277)

### Measurement Marks
- Marks every 100mm along edges
- Bottom edge: marks at x=100, 200
- Top edge: marks at x=100, 200
- Left edge: marks at y=100, 200
- Right edge: marks at y=100, 200
- Mark size: 5mm

## Usage
Place this template as "A4.dwg" or "A4.3dm" in the Resources/DWG folder.
The component will automatically load and use this template for A4 frames.
